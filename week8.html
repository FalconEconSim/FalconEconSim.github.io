Week 8 · Intermediate Microeconomics

Welfare, Monopoly & Price Discrimination

Prof. Naveen Sunder

This week we measure how well markets perform: consumer and producer surplus, deadweight loss, and the gains from trade and tariffs. We then turn to monopoly, where the two-step rule (MR = MC, then up to demand) sets price above marginal cost and the Lerner index measures the markup. We finish with price discrimination, from perfect first-degree pricing to block pricing and market separation, and the regulator's dilemma for natural monopolies.
