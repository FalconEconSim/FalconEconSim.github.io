Two directions for the figures, and the new animations

Week 11 (externalities, cap-and-trade, positive externalities, public goods, Coase), rebuilt two ways. Same models and default numbers as the current week 11 page. Both work on a laptop and a phone.

Figure redesigns

  1. Option A

    Predict, then see

    Each figure asks a question first, and the student answers on the graph itself: tap where the market lands, drag the cheapest split of cleanup, draw the social benefit curve with a finger. Reveal then animates the real answer over their guess, scores how close they were, and explains why. After that they can explore freely or try a fresh set of numbers for practice.

    Try first: Figure 11.3, draw the curve, then press Reveal. Then 11.5.

    Open
  2. Option B

    Touch the economics

    No sliders anywhere. Students change things by grabbing the graph: pull the market point up the demand curve, lift a curve by its handle, drag each neighbor's benefit curve and watch the total update. Handles click onto the key points (the optimum, the market outcome) and say why. Numbers sit on the graph next to what they measure.

    Try first: Figure 11.1, pull the gold point up the demand curve until it clicks onto Q* = 35.
