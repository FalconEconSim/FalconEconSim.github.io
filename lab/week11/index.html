Week 11, five ways

The same five figures (Pigouvian tax, cap-and-trade, positive externality, public goods, Coase), rebuilt five different ways. Same economics and default numbers as the live week 11 page. Try each on a laptop and on a phone, then pick a direction, or mix them.

  1. 1

    Manim Live

    The figures look and move like the films: a narration column that rewrites itself, smooth eased changes, one handle and one or two big controls per figure, and a "Play the story" button.

    Try first: press Play the story on 11.1, then grab the red SMC handle halfway through.

    Open
  2. 2

    Touch the economics

    No sliders at all. Grab the graph itself: pull the market point, lift a curve by its handle, drag each neighbor's benefit curve. Handles snap to the optimum and say why. Numbers sit on the graph.

    Try first: on a phone, pull the gold point in 11.1 up the demand curve until it clicks onto Q* = 35.

    Open
  3. 3

    Scrollytelling

    Each figure is a short scroll story. The graph stays pinned while steps scroll past and build it up, and scrolling back runs it in reverse. The last step hands you a couple of big controls.

    Try first: scroll slowly through 11.1 and back up; then 11.4 on a phone.

    Open
  4. 4

    Living sentences

    No control panels: the numbers in the text are the controls. Drag a number sideways (or tap it on a phone) and the sentence and the graph rewrite together. Hover a number to light up what it measures.

    Try first: in 11.1, drag the gold tax number slowly from $0 past $20.

    Open
  5. 5

    Predict, then see

    Each figure asks a question first. You answer by dragging or drawing on the graph, then Reveal animates the real answer over your guess and explains it. Then explore, or try a fresh version.

    Try first: 11.4, guess what the three neighbors would pay together, then reveal.
