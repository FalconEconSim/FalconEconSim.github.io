# Site audit against the references

Phase 3. Every finding below is anchored to a screenshot in
`C:\MicroSite\review\shots\` and to a line of our source, and is mapped to the
revamp priority order:

- **P1** layout & navigation structure
- **P2** how visualizations sit next to text
- **P3** motion / animation polish
- **P4** typography & whitespace

Severity: **S1** breaks the page's job for a student · **S2** materially cheapens
or clutters it · **S3** finish.

Baseline runs: `shots/baseline/` (35 pages @1366 + @390), `shots/slice-ours/`,
`shots/slice-ours-m/`, `shots/slice-ask/`.

---

## 0. Site-wide findings

These recur on every page and are listed once. Each is severe enough that page
sections below assume they are being fixed.

### SW-1 · S1 · P1: Three incompatible design systems ship side by side

Measured with a class-vocabulary diff across the seven viz pages:

| Page | System | Container | Section wrapper | Controls | Readout | max-width |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `week2-viz`, `week3-viz` | **A** | `.viz-wrapper` | `.section-block` | `.ctrl-group` | `.stat-row` / `.stat-box` | 1520px, left sidebar TOC |
| `week4-viz` … `week8-viz` | **B** | `.cvs-wrap` | `.fig-section` | `.viz-ctrl-row` | `.viz-readout` | 1400px, boxed `.toc` |
| `week1.html` | **C** | n/a | `.section` | n/a | n/a | 920px |

Compare `shots/slice-ask/week2-viz-html__1440-s0.png` (grey page, left
`§1…§9` sidebar, 40px title, "KEY IDEA" callouts) with
`shots/slice-ours/week5-viz-html__1440-s1.png` (white page, boxed TOC, 20px
title, mono panel bars). A student moving from Week 3 to Week 4 lands on what
looks like a different website. Weeks 2 and 3 are also the only pages that ever
got a proper reading measure; weeks 4-8 never did.

MLU ships six articles on one template (`css/styles.css` + one per-article
stylesheet that only adds figure-specific rules). Seeing Theory ships six
chapters on `css/chapter-style.css` with a single per-chapter background colour
changing.

**Fix:** one system. Take the *reading structure* from A (sidebar section list,
numbered sections, real measure) and drop B's container chrome entirely.

### SW-3 · S2 · P1/P4: Seventeen variants of the same grey rounded box

`shared.css` defines "1px solid var(--rule) + border-radius 6-9px + off-white
fill" seventeen times: `.q-card:162`, `.eq-row:218`, `.accordion:181`,
`.pq-item:247`, `.video-item:278`, `.warning-box:236`, `.app-box:205`,
`.how-to-use:496`, `.summary-notice:265`, `.assump-card:465`,
`.props-list li:470`, `.week-card:507`, `.flowchart:226`, `.fc-node:227`,
`.fc-result:233`, plus `.toc` and `.viz-readout` on each viz page.

MLU's entire landing page has one bordered element: `0.5px solid #232f3e` on a
thumbnail (`css/styles.css:366-370`); its `.article-card` has no border, radius,
shadow, padding or background at all (`css/styles.css:353-358`).

Fig 5.1 alone nests six of ours: `.fig-section` → `#v51-form-desc` → `.cvs-wrap`
→ `.panel-label` → `.viz-ctrl-row` → `.viz-readout`
(`week5-viz.html:135-180`).

### SW-4 · S2 · P4: Vertical rhythm is roughly 8× too tight

`.section { padding: 1.1rem 0 }` (`shared.css:155`) ·
`.week-grid { gap: 0.7rem }` (`shared.css:506`) ·
`.accordion-list { gap: 0.32rem }` (`shared.css:180`) ·
`.hub-list { gap: 0.45rem 0.9rem }` (`week5.html:18`) ·
`.eq-list { gap: 0.32rem }` (`shared.css:217`).

Largest routine gap on the site: **11px**. MLU:
`.articles-container { row-gap: 5rem; column-gap: 5rem }`
(`css/styles.css:341-351`) = **80px**, and `section { margin-bottom: 24vh }`
in the article template.

### SW-5 · S2 · P1: Emoji are the icon system

`week5.html:108,118,128,142,152` uses 📖 📑 📊 ✏️ 🃏 at `font-size: 1.85rem`.
Same on all eight hubs and on the slides pages
(`shots/baseline/week2-slides/laptop__full.png`). Emoji render differently on
every OS, carry no brand, and are the clearest single "generated by an AI"
signal on the site. MLU hand-draws its iconography; Seeing Theory renders live
mini-visualisations as its chapter icons
(`shots/ref-st/basic-probability-index-html__1440.png`).

### SW-6 · S2 · P4: Every page is `#ffffff`

`shared.css:65`. None of the three references uses white: MLU precision-recall
`rgb(241,243,243)`, MLU train-test-validation `rgba(252,244,232,.855)`, Seeing
Theory chapter 1 `rgb(214,239,250)` with a different tint per chapter.

### SW-7 · S3 · P3: Decorative motion, no functional motion

`assets/polish.js` + `shared.css:665-736` ship a full-viewport film-grain
overlay (`body::after`, `z-index: 9990`), a cursor-following radial spotlight on
cards, and a hover lift. None of it explains anything. Meanwhile the ~60
interactive figures change state instantly with no transition at all, and the
delta readouts (`assets/deltabar.js`) snap.

Setosa's rule is the inverse: `transition: 0.25s all` on the thing you are
dragging (`ordinary-least-squares-regression/style.less:6-15`), and MLU animates
a point's radius up and back to direct attention
(`.transition().delay(1300).attr("r",12).transition().attr("r",9.5)`,
`bias-variance/js.63b32ab2.js`).

### SW-8 · S2 · P1: The chat FAB collides with content on every page

`shots/baseline/index/mobile__full.png` (covers the Week 02 card),
`shots/baseline/week3-practice/laptop__full.png` (covers the answer inputs),
`shots/slice-ours-m/week5-viz-html__390-s1.png` (covers the chart). The pill
"Stuck? Ask the Econ AI (24/7)" plus a "24/7" badge on the button is the same
claim twice.
