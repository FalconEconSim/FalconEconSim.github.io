Sixteen labels across two panels, three level curves each, a live line and a faded original. Every label names an object, not a coordinate, and the contact point is computed from that object each frame. Press Sweep both and watch where the dashed lines land.

Measured over one full sweep: 0 of 324 leaders ended more than 6px from the thing they name, worst gap 1px, 1,364 leaders drawn. On the current page the same measurement gives 72 adrift of 328, worst 21px.

How it works

Answer the problem by removing most of the leaders. Labels are placed on a tighter ring, so most of them end up close enough to their object to read as attached, and no line is drawn for those.

A leader appears only where a label had to be pushed away, and then it is the shortest link. Fewer lines on the figure, and the ones that remain carry meaning.

What it buysBy far the cleanest figure. A leader becomes a signal that something unusual happened rather than background noise.
What it costsLabels sit nearer the ink they are dodging, so they crowd the curves more, and "close enough to read as attached" is a judgement that will not hold on every figure.
