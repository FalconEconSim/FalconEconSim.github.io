Course AI — experiments

Three takes on the same RAG backend (Pinecone + Gemini via a Cloudflare Worker). The goal: when the bot can answer, point students to the exact spot on the course site; when it can’t, say so plainly and show no sources. The live-preview panes only auto-highlight when this page is opened from falconeconsim.github.io (same-origin).

Split-view. Ask on the left; the right pane loads the cited course page and scrolls to & highlights the passage the answer came from. Click the source chips to jump between citations. If the bot can’t answer, the pane stays empty — no misleading links.
