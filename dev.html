Course AI · Chatbot experiments · live page preview
Three takes on the RAG chatbot (Pinecone + Gemini via a Cloudflare Worker): a split-view that loads the cited course page beside the chat and scrolls to & highlights the exact passage; a compact embeddable widget with “show me on the page” citation pills + follow-up chips (also embedded on the Week 3 Claude viz page); and the classic baseline. Weekly Recap Video & Ask the Course AI · Prototype
