Study Materials
Textbook
Read the full Week 9 chapter covering linear vs non-linear pricing, the optimal two-part tariff (set the usage charge at MC and the fixed fee at consumer surplus) and its profit-doubling result, pricing to heterogeneous consumers and information rents, natural monopoly and the regulator's four regimes, cross-subsidisation, and a first course in game theory: Nash equilibrium, dominant strategies, the prisoner's dilemma, and coordination games.

Three interactive tools: the two-part pricing benchmark (watch profit double as the usage charge falls to MC and the fixed fee sweeps up the consumer-surplus triangle), the natural monopoly regulatory dilemma across all four regimes (unregulated, MC pricing, AC pricing, two-part), and an editable prisoner's-dilemma payoff matrix that solves itself by the circle (best-response) method.
