Unit 1 Consumer theory

01

Foundations of Microeconomics

The calculus you need, opportunity cost and sunk cost, and the first look at utility, indifference curves and the MRS.
02

Indifference Curves & the Consumer Optimum

Budget constraints, tangency, and where a consumer actually lands. Cobb-Douglas worked end to end.
03

Demand Curves & Comparative Statics

Where a demand curve comes from, and how a price change splits into a substitution effect and an income effect.
04

Elasticity & Consumer Welfare

How responsive demand is, why that decides whether a price rise raises revenue, and what surplus measures.

Unit 2 Producer theory

05

Production Theory

The firm as a machine for turning inputs into output: total and marginal product, isoquants, MRTS and returns to scale.
06

Cost Theory

From the production function to the cost curves: isocost lines, the shapes of TC, AC and MC, and the long-run envelope.
07

Profit Maximization & the Competitive Firm

Why MR = MC, when a firm should shut down, and how the individual supply curve emerges from the cost curves.

Unit 3 Market structure

08

Welfare, Monopoly & Price Discrimination

Consumer and producer surplus, what monopoly destroys, and how a firm that can charge different prices behaves.
09

Non-Linear Pricing, Natural Monopoly & Game Theory

Two-part pricing, the natural monopoly problem, and the first strategic interactions.
10

Oligopoly Models & Spatial Competition

Cournot, Stackelberg and Bertrand, and what happens when location itself is the strategy.

Unit 4 Welfare & extensions

11

Public Goods & Externalities

Public goods and the free-rider problem, externalities and deadweight loss, and the policy tools that correct them.
12

Adverse Selection & Moral Hazard

Hidden information and hidden action: the lemons model, signalling and screening, and the principal-agent problem.
