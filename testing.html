Visual Tests: Sizes, Labels & Force Placement

All canvases use a cost-curves figure: MC (red), ATC (blue), AVC (green), MR=P (purple), with 5 force-placed labels. Tell me which variant in each section looks best and I will apply it everywhere.

Currently figures fill the full container (up to 1300 px wide → huge squares). Test 4 different max-width caps. Which size fits comfortably on your screen?

Very compact. Two figures easily fit side-by-side on any screen.

Section 3Force Label Approaches

4 different collision-avoidance algorithms. All use 12 px font on a 460 px canvas. Which avoids lines and other labels best?

A — Current: spring-back + mild push
150 iter, push 0.5×, spring pulls label 5% back to anchor each iter, line-repel 0.4×. Labels stay close to anchor but may still overlap lines.
B — Strong push, no spring, pre-offset
Labels pre-positioned 30 px away from nearest line before iteration. 250 iter, push 0.8×, no spring, line-repel 0.7×. Labels float freely; connector shows anchor.
C — Smart pre-placement (away from curves)
Each label pre-placed 38 px in the direction away from the average of its 20 nearest line points. Then 90 iter light resolve. Labels start on the right side from the beginning.
D — Best-of-8 compass positions
For each label, tests 8 compass directions (R = 42 px). Picks the position with fewest overlapping labels + line points. Then 70 iter fine-tune. Structured, predictable placement.

Section 4Label Visual Styles

4 ways to draw the label and connector. All use Approach B forces + 12 px font on 460 px canvas. Which looks cleanest?

A — Current: gray leader + white rect
Thin gray (30% opaque) leader line. White rect background. No border. Text in curve color.
B — Colored leader + colored border box
2 px leader in label color. White rect with 1 px colored border. More visually tied to the curve.
C — Pill label, no leader
White pill shape with subtle shadow. No connector line — label floats over the canvas. Cleanest look.
D — Dot at anchor + colored leader
Small filled circle at the curve anchor, 2 px colored leader, white rect label. Makes the exact anchor point explicit.

Section 5Fig 6.10 Shading Fix

AC = q + 9/q. Efficient scale at q* = 3. At q = 1 the AC hits the y-axis top; for q < 1 it exits the chart. The green “economies of scale” region should shade from the left edge of the plot to q*, but currently it starts where AC first becomes visible.

A — Current (buggy): green starts late
Green shading only covers q values where AC ≤ acmax. The left strip (where AC is above the chart) is unshaded even though it is in the economies region.
B — Fixed: full left edge shading
AC values above acmax are clamped to the chart top. The path starts at the x-axis under q = 0.3, goes up to the top edge, follows the clamped curve in, then closes at q*. The clip region handles the boundary.
