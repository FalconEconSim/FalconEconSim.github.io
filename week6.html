Cost Theory

Learning the Material
Lecture Slides & Videos
Download the clean or annotated slide decks (three parts) and watch the fourteen asynchronous lecture videos covering basic cost concepts, total cost functions, economic profit, short-run vs long-run costs, average and marginal costs, economies of scale, and the LRAC envelope.

Textbook
Read the full Week 6 chapter covering cost minimisation, the isocost line, short-run and long-run cost functions, average and marginal costs, economies of scale, and the LRAC envelope - including all derivations and key diagrams from lecture.
›
Interactive Visualizations
Seven interactive tools: the cost minimisation sandbox, two-panel cost derivation, bang-for-the-buck reallocation engine, SR vs LR cost curve stack, LRAC envelope builder, minimum wage automation simulator, and efficient scale finder.

Practice
Graded Homework
Submit your graded problem set for Week 6 on Brightspace. Complete and submit before the deadline to receive full credit.

Flashcards
20 cards covering economic vs accounting profit, the isocost line, cost minimisation (bang-for-the-buck rule), all five SR cost curves, ATC minimum, the LRAC envelope theorem, economies and diseconomies of scale, and the common IRS/economies-of-scale distinction.
